# **My Work Desk — V 2.0 (Beta)**

### **Architected by \[santio.ignatius\]**

A standalone, private, and local-first productivity suite designed for high-focus deep work. This application is distributed for **FREE** to empower individual workflows without the overhead of cloud-dependency or privacy concerns.

## **🧭 Project Roadmap & Evolution**

As a product developed through rigorous systems-thinking, the versioning reflects a journey from conceptual prototype to a public utility.

### **Phase 1: Foundation (v0.1 — v1.5)**

* **Status:** Completed  
* **Focus:** Logic validation, "Desk" vs "Board" architecture, and CSS framework integration.

### **Phase 2: Launch & Resilience (V 2.0 Beta)**

* **Status:** CURRENT  
* **Focus:** Public Beta release, macOS/Safari optimization, and establishing the stable local-storage engine.

### **Phase 3: Workflow Refinement (V 2.1.0)**

* **Status:** PLANNED  
* **Focus:** Minor workflow enhancements, logic polishing, and community-reported bug fixes.

## **🔒 Privacy & Data Policy**

* **Zero-Server Architecture:** Your data never leaves your machine.  
* **Local-First:** All notes, desks, and layouts are saved exclusively in your browser’s localStorage.  
* **User Responsibility:** Users are encouraged to use the built-in **Backup** feature to safeguard their data before clearing browser caches.
